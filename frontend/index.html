The Core Challenge

Why Traditional Crop Selling Limits Farmer Income

Smallholders and producer groups often sell in the dark without comparative visibility of alternative channels, true transport costs, and buyer credentials.

Information Asymmetry

Farmers lack real-time visibility into mandi price trends and expected arrivals, leading to hurried sales right at harvest peaks when local prices hit seasonal lows.

Impact: Forced distress selling

Weak Bargaining & Middlemen

Without direct access to food processors and institutional buyers, farmers rely solely on local intermediaries who capture the majority of the value chain margin.

Impact: 15–30% value loss

Hidden Logistics & Storage Costs

A higher headline mandi price 150 km away may actually result in lower net take-home cash once freight, handling, and storage losses are accounted for.

Impact: Misleading gross prices

Volume & Quality Mismatch

Institutional buyers require consistent Grade A volumes that individual smallholders cannot supply alone, while FPO aggregation tools remain fragmented.

Impact: Buyer sourcing friction

Payment Reliability Uncertainty

Selling to unknown buyers risks delayed settlement cycles and informal deductions without clear dispute or grievance resolution mechanisms.

Impact: Credit and default risk

How KisanLink Connects Farm to Market

An explainable, step-by-step pipeline designed for both individual farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).

STEP 01

Create Crop Lot

Declare your crop commodity, quantity (e.g. 10 QTL), self-assessed Grade A/B/C, farm location, and availability date.

STEP 02

Market Intelligence

System automatically scans APMC mandi rates, daily arrival volumes, price trends, and nearby storage availability.

STEP 03

Net Realisation Engine

Calculates true take-home value by deducting estimated freight and storage from gross offers to find genuine best yield.

STEP 04

Verified Buyer Matching

Matches your lot against active demand from food processors and institutional aggregators using weighted compatibility scores.

STEP 05

Digital Offer & Terms

Receive clear digital purchase offers with guaranteed pickup timeline, agreed grade specs, and transparent settlement terms.

STEP 06

Transparent Transaction

Track status from Offered → Accepted → Logistics → Settlement, with built-in grievance protection.
